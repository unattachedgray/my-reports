## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**TurboQuant Makes 35B Models Viable on 24GB**
Google's KV cache compression (6x, zero accuracy loss) means a 35B model with 64K context can now fit on a single 24GB card instead of requiring 48GB. Community PyTorch implementation on GitHub. However, note a production caveat: combining TurboQuant with speculative decoding can be counterproductive due to higher CUDA kernel launch rates.
[O-Mega: TurboQuant Guide](https://o-mega.ai/articles/google-turboquant-the-2026-llm-compression-guide) | [Stark Insider](https://www.starkinsider.com/2026/03/google-turboquant-llm-compression-less-memory.html)

**Speculative Decoding: 2-3x Faster, Now Production Standard**
Speculative decoding went from research to production in 2026, built into vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, and most serving frameworks. On AMD MI300X, combining FP8 quantization with speculative decoding showed 3.6x total improvement (multiplicative, not additive). For RTX 4090 users: EAGLE-3 draft models give the best single-GPU speedups.
[PremAI: Speculative Decoding Guide](https://blog.premai.io/speculative-decoding-2-3x-faster-llm-inference-2026/) | [RunPod: Inference Optimization](https://www.runpod.io/blog/llm-inference-optimization-techniques-reduce-latency-cost)

**RTX 4090 Inference Benchmarks: Where You Stand**
Fresh numbers: 128 tok/s on 8B models (llama.cpp), 4,300 tok/s prompt processing on Llama 3.1 8B, SDXL 1024x1024 in 5.5-7 seconds. Qwen 3 30B-A3B MoE hits 196 tok/s. The 4090 remains the best consumer GPU for AI — nothing under $2K touches it.
[Local AI Master: Best GPUs 2026](https://localaimaster.com/blog/best-gpus-for-ai-2025) | [Compute Market: GPU Guide](https://www.compute-market.com/blog/best-gpu-for-ai-2026)

**VRAM Optimization Stack: The March 2026 Playbook**
Layer these techniques: GPTQ int4 (4x VRAM reduction, 1-2% accuracy loss), PagedAttention + FlashAttention-2 (30-40% savings), NVFP4/FP8 in llama.cpp (35% faster tokens), gradient checkpointing for fine-tuning. QLoRA still the king for training — fine-tune up to 20B params comfortably on 24GB.
[Dasroot: VRAM Optimization](https://dasroot.net/posts/2026/03/optimizing-vram-usage-local-llms/) | [Clarifai: llama.cpp Guide](https://www.clarifai.com/blog/ilama.cpp)

**On-Device LLMs: State of the Union 2026**
Meta Research published a comprehensive survey of on-device LLM deployment covering quantization, pruning, architecture search, and speculative decoding for edge hardware. Key takeaway: the gap between cloud and on-device capability is narrowing faster than expected, with 3B-active MoE models matching 14B dense quality at edge-viable speeds.
[Meta Research: On-Device LLMs](https://v-chandra.github.io/on-device-llms/)
