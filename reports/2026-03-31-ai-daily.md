## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"The Era of Evaluation, Not Evangelism" — Stanford AI Faculty**
Experts across CS, medicine, law, and economics converge: 2026 demands rigor over hype. The question shifts from "Can AI do this?" to "How well, at what cost, and for whom?" This applies equally to frontier labs (can revenue justify $650B+ capex?) and hobbyists (does a local agent actually save time?).
[Stanford: AI Predictions 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/stanford-ai-experts-predict-what-will-happen-in-2026)

**"Hope Centers on Reliability, Not New Models" — Governance Analysts**
The most anticipated advances in 2026 aren't model families — they're security, observability, provenance, evaluation of long-horizon behavior, and governance frameworks. Enterprises want agents that work reliably within compliance boundaries, not agents that score higher on benchmarks.
[ODSC: 2026 AI Outlook](https://odsc.medium.com/2026-ai-outlook-how-agents-context-and-governance-will-shape-real-world-ai-0f76d2c716f6)

**"Broader Participation Is Healthy Normalization" — Market Commentary**
Investment analysts argue that AI's sector rotation in 2026 — from pure-play model companies to infrastructure, governance, and vertical applications — represents healthy market maturation rather than decline. The real risk isn't a bubble pop but a slow grind where capex outpaces revenue for years.
[Carnegie: March 2026 Market Commentary](https://blog.carnegieinvest.com/monthly-market-commentary-march-2026)

**"Physical AI Hits Mainstream in 2026" — Industry Forecasters**
Robotics ($1.2B raised in one week), autonomous vehicles, drones, and wearables are entering the market. The convergence of capable models, cheaper compute, and better sensors means physical AI moves from lab to product this year.
[IBM: AI Tech Trends 2026](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/ai-tech-trends-predictions-2026)

## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**LTX-2: 4K AI Video Generation on a Single 4090**
LTX-2 generates native 4K at 50fps with synchronized audio, up to 10 seconds per clip. A 10-second 4K clip takes 9-12 minutes on RTX 4090 with 30-36 diffusion steps. Open source and locally runnable — no cloud API needed.
[AI Films: LTX-2 Guide](https://studio.aifilms.ai/blog/ltx-2-open-source-ai-video-model)

**DeepSeek-R1 14B/32B: Frontier Reasoning on Consumer Hardware**
The model that changed open-source reasoning. The 14B distill is the sweet spot for most users; the 32B distill approaches frontier quality on math and logic, running comfortably on a 4090. Both available via Ollama with one-command setup.
[Hardwarepedia: Best Local Models March 2026](https://hardwarepedia.com/blog/best-open-source-ai-models-to-run-locally-2026)

**AWQ Hits 741 tok/s — GPU-Optimized Quants Beat GGUF**
With Marlin kernels, AWQ achieves 741 tok/s vs GGUF's lower throughput on NVIDIA hardware. For RTX 4090 users running vLLM or TensorRT-LLM, switching from GGUF to AWQ provides measurable speedups. GPTQ at 712 tok/s is the runner-up.
[Starmorph: Local LLM Inference 2026](https://blog.starmorph.com/blog/local-llm-inference-tools-guide)

**EdgeMoE: Fit More Experts in Less VRAM**
EdgeMoE partitions MoE experts to external storage and fetches on activation — reducing memory 5-18% while improving inference 1.2-2.7x. Ideal for running large MoE models (Llama 4 Scout, Qwen3-Coder-Next) on 24GB when full loading is tight.
[Meta Research: On-Device LLMs](https://v-chandra.github.io/on-device-llms/)

**BitNet b1.58: The 1.58-Bit Frontier**
Experimental research uses only three possible weight values (-1, 0, +1), resulting in 71.4% lower energy consumption. Not production-ready yet, but a glimpse of where quantization is heading — models that fit in a fraction of current VRAM at usable quality.
[Dasroot: VRAM Optimization](https://dasroot.net/posts/2026/03/optimizing-vram-usage-local-llms/)

**Best Local Coding Models: March 2026 Rankings**
Qwen3-Coder-Next (80B MoE, 3B active) at 40-60+ tok/s leads for agentic coding. DeepSeek-R1 32B for reasoning. Llama 4 Scout Q4 for general tasks at ~45 tok/s. All run on a single 4090 via Ollama.
[Local AI Master: Best Coding Models](https://localaimaster.com/models/best-local-ai-coding-models)
