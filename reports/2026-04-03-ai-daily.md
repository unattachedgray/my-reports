## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**Gemma 4: Google's Best Open Model, Now on Your 4090**
Gemma 4 26B MoE runs locally via Ollama with NVIDIA RTX optimization. Apache 2.0 license — use however you want. Native function calling means you can build local agents with tool use out of the box. The E4B variant is optimized for on-device, and the 26B MoE should fit comfortably on 24GB with Q4 quantization.
[9to5Google: Gemma 4](https://9to5google.com/2026/04/02/google-gemma-4/) | [NVIDIA: Gemma 4 RTX](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/nvidia-optimizes-google-s-gemma-4-models-for-local-rtx-ai)

**Qwen 3 32B Remains the RTX 4090 Champion**
Even with Gemma 4 landing, Qwen 3 32B (Q4) stays the top pick for 24GB — fits comfortably with excellent quality at ~20 tok/s. The RTX 4090 delivers 40-70+ tok/s on common models. Full speed chart: 7B Q4 = 80-100 tok/s, 13B Q4 = 50-70, 30B Q4 = 20-35, 70B Q2 = 15-25.
[ToolHalla: Best LLMs for RTX 4090](https://toolhalla.ai/blog/best-local-llms-rtx-4090-2026)

**EXL2: Variable Bits-Per-Weight Quantization**
EXL2 allocates more bits to layers sensitive to precision loss and fewer to tolerant layers — smarter than uniform 4-bit everywhere. On ExLlamaV2 (fastest single-user NVIDIA inference), this delivers better quality-per-VRAM-byte than GGUF or GPTQ. Worth trying if you want maximum quality from 24GB.
[LocalLLM: Quantization Guide](https://localllm.in/blog/quantization-explained) | [Local AI Master: GGUF vs GPTQ vs AWQ](https://localaimaster.com/blog/quantization-explained)

**Context Kills VRAM — How to Manage It**
KV cache is the dominant variable memory cost. A 7B model at 4K context uses ~1.5GB for KV cache; at 32K it's ~12GB. TurboQuant (3-bit KV, 6x compression) and sliding window attention are the key techniques. For long-context work on 24GB, prefer models with native sliding window (Mistral) or use KV cache quantization.
[Medium: Context Kills VRAM](https://medium.com/@lyx_62906/context-kills-vram-how-to-run-llms-on-consumer-gpus-a785e8035632)

**RTX 4090 vs 5090 for AI: Is It Worth Upgrading?**
The RTX 5090 delivers ~67% improvement over 4090 and its 32GB VRAM runs 70B Q4 more comfortably. But the 4090 at current used prices remains the best value for local AI. Only upgrade if you routinely need 70B+ models or long-context 32B inference.
[Dev.to: Local AI Hardware 2026](https://dev.to/axrisi/the-local-ai-hardware-guide-2026-4mk) | [Compute Market: Best Hardware](https://www.compute-market.com/blog/best-hardware-ai-agents-local-2026)
