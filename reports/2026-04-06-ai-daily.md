# AI Daily Briefing — 2026-04-06

## PART 1 — AI News of the Day

**DeepSeek V4: 1 Trillion Parameters, Apache 2.0, Running on Huawei Chips**
The most consequential open-source release of 2026. DeepSeek V4 is a ~1T parameter MoE model with ~37B active parameters per token, 1M-token context (Engram conditional memory), and native multimodal generation (text, image, video). Apache 2.0 license. Trained on Huawei chips — the first frontier model running entirely on non-NVIDIA silicon. 81% on SWE-bench, $0.30/MTok API pricing — potentially 50x cheaper than comparable proprietary models.
[NxCode: DeepSeek V4 Specs](https://www.nxcode.io/resources/news/deepseek-v4-release-specs-benchmarks-2026) | [Digital Applied: DeepSeek V4](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/deepseek-v4-trillion-parameter-open-source-multimodal)

**Tariffs Threaten $75-100B in AI Infrastructure Costs**
CSIS analysis: current and proposed tariff policies could add $75-100B in AI infrastructure costs over 5 years, equivalent to 15-20 fewer hyperscale facilities. However, selective tariff exemptions for AI hardware are cushioning the blow. AI infrastructure spending still expected to hit $650B in 2026, up from $410B in 2025.
[CSIS: Tariffs vs AI Buildout](https://www.csis.org/analysis/how-tariffs-could-derail-united-states-3-trillion-ai-buildout) | [Apricitas: Tariff Exemption](https://www.apricitas.io/p/the-tariff-exemption-behind-the-ai)

**Gemini 3.1 Pro Leads 13 of 16 Benchmarks — At 1/3 the API Cost of GPT-5.4**
Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro ties GPT-5.4 Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index while costing roughly a third as much. Combined with Gemma 4 (Apache 2.0 open models) and Flash-Lite ($0.25/M tokens), Google is executing a full-spectrum price/capability strategy.
[Crescendo: AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**45,000 Tech Jobs Cut in Q1 2026 — AI Cited in 20%+**
The workforce impact becomes visible. More than 45,000 tech jobs eliminated in Q1 alone, with companies explicitly citing AI as the reason for at least 20% of those reductions. The "Ghost GDP" thesis — AI automating jobs faster than new roles appear — is moving from essay to earnings call.
[Domino Tech: AI Trends Q1 Roundup](https://www.dominotech.net/2026/04/01/ai-trends-q1-roundup/)

**Georgia AI Bills Reach Governor's Desk**
Three AI bills: SB 540 (chatbot disclosure + child safety), SR 789 (AI study committee), SB 444 (prohibits insurance decisions based solely on AI). State-level regulation continues advancing faster than federal frameworks.
[Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-april3-2026)

**Claude Code Holds 54% Market Share in AI Coding Tools**
Cursor 3 release (agent-first IDE) is framed as a direct response to Claude Code's dominance. The AI coding tool market is consolidating around two paradigms: agent-fleet management (Cursor 3) vs. single-agent deep integration (Claude Code).
[Paddo.dev: April Roundup](https://paddo.dev/blog/ai-roundup-april-2026/)

## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"DeepSeek V4 Is the Real Inflection Point" — Open Source Analysis**
Not because it's the smartest model (Claude Opus still leads on coding), but because it's potentially the cheapest frontier model by 50x, the first running entirely on non-NVIDIA hardware, and fully open-source under Apache 2.0. If performance claims hold, DeepSeek V4 reshapes the economic model of AI infrastructure globally.
[QverLabs: DeepSeek V4 Analysis](https://qverlabs.com/blog/deepseek-v4-trillion-parameter-multimodal-ai) | [Particula: Open-Source Disruption](https://particula.tech/blog/deepseek-v4-qwen-open-source-ai-disruption)

**"Tariff Exemptions Are the Hidden Story of the AI Boom" — Economic Analysis**
The massive tariff carveout for AI hardware is much of the reason America's stock market keeps surging despite the trade war. Without these exemptions, the $3T AI buildout would face crippling cost increases. The policy choice: protect AI infrastructure spending at the expense of other industries.
[Apricitas: Tariff Exemption](https://www.apricitas.io/p/the-tariff-exemption-behind-the-ai)

**"Agent-First Is the Only Interface That Matters Now" — Developer Analysis**
Cursor 3 rebuilt its entire UI around agent fleets. Gemma 4 was designed for agentic workflows on-device. Every major release in April pushes toward agents rather than chat. The consensus: the chat-with-AI paradigm is being replaced by the delegate-to-agents paradigm.
[Paddo.dev: April's First 72 Hours](https://paddo.dev/blog/ai-roundup-april-2026/)

**"Federal Preemption Is Winning" — Regulatory Analysis**
The 2026 National AI Policy Framework establishes federal preemption over state AI regulations, with the DOJ AI Litigation Task Force actively challenging state laws. This materially reduces compliance uncertainty for multi-state AI deployments, favoring rapid scaling over cautious state-by-state compliance.
[Valere Labs: AI Policy Framework](https://valerelabs.medium.com/the-2026-national-ai-policy-framework-changing-the-regulatory-calculus-for-ai-value-creation-692f5126a89e)

## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**Gemma 4 as Local Coding Assistant: "Might Replace Your AI IDE Subscription"**
Detailed user report of running Gemma 4 26B MoE locally for coding. The verdict: with native function calling and NVIDIA optimization, it's competitive with paid AI IDE subscriptions for most coding tasks. 64-119 tok/s on consumer GPUs. Integrates with Android Studio for local agentic coding.
[Medium: Gemma 4 Local Coding](https://medium.com/synthetic-futures/i-ran-googles-new-gemma-4-as-a-local-coding-assistant-it-might-replace-your-monthly-ai-ide-82c4c85e0e95) | [Android Dev Blog: Gemma 4 + Studio](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/04/android-studio-supports-gemma-4-local.html)

**DeepSeek V4: What It Means for Home Labs**
The 37B active parameter slice of the 1T MoE should be quantizable for 24GB VRAM, similar to how Llama 4 Scout (17B active, 109B total) runs on a 4090. If DeepSeek follows their usual open-source pattern, GGUF quantizations will appear on HuggingFace within days of release. A trillion-parameter model on a consumer GPU was unthinkable a year ago.
[FindSkill: DeepSeek V4 Specs](https://findskill.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-release-date-specs/)

**AMD Day-0 Support for Gemma 4**
AMD announced immediate support for Gemma 4 on AMD processors and GPUs. If you have an AMD GPU, this closes a gap — Gemma 4 now runs optimized on both NVIDIA and AMD consumer hardware. ROCm users finally get a frontier-class model with day-0 optimization.
[AMD: Gemma 4 Day-0 Support](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/resources/technical-articles/2026/day-0-support-for-gemma-4-on-amd-processors-and-gpus.html)

**GPU for AI Agent Self-Hosting: VRAM + Context Math**
New guide for sizing GPUs for agentic workloads (not just chat): agents need more VRAM due to long context, tool outputs, and multi-turn reasoning. Rule of thumb for RTX 4090: 32B Q4 model + 32K context = ~22GB VRAM. Drop to 8K context for comfortable headroom with tool-heavy agents.
[CraftRigs: GPU for AI Agent Self-Hosting](https://craftrigs.com/articles/88-what-gpu-self-host-ai-agent-2026/)

**RTX 4090 Speed Reference — April 6 Edition**
Gemma 4 26B MoE: 64-119 tok/s. Gemma 4 31B Dense: 30-34 tok/s. Qwen 3 30B-A3B: 196 tok/s. 8B models: 145 tok/s. 13B Q4: 50-70. 30B Q4: 20-35. 70B Q2: 15-25. ExLlamaV2 = fastest single-user NVIDIA framework.
