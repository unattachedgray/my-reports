# AI Daily Briefing — 2026-04-07

## PART 1 — AI News of the Day

**Anthropic Signs 3.5 GW TPU Deal with Google + Broadcom — Revenue Hits $30B Run Rate**
Anthropic's most significant compute commitment ever: 3.5 gigawatts of next-gen TPU capacity via Google/Broadcom, coming online 2027. Run-rate revenue now exceeds $30B — up from $9B at end of 2025 — a 3.3x increase in under 16 months. Most infrastructure will be US-based. The deal was announced alongside Broadcom's confirmation they're already supplying Anthropic with 1 GW of current-gen TPUs.
[Anthropic: Google Broadcom Partnership](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) | [The Register: $30B Run Rate](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/07/broadcom_google_chip_deal_anthropic_customer/)

**Q1 2026 Venture: $300B Record — AI Got 80% of It**
AI received $242 billion of the $300B total — 80% of all global venture funding in Q1. The four largest rounds ever recorded were all in Q1 2026: OpenAI ($122B), Anthropic ($30B), xAI ($20B), Waymo ($16B). These four alone account for 65% of all global venture investment in the quarter.
[Crunchbase: $300B Record](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/)

**Google Drops Veo 3.1 Fast Pricing + Launches Veo 3.1 Lite**
Price cut on Veo 3.1 Fast effective today. New Veo 3.1 Lite generates video from text/images at <50% the cost of Fast with no speed reduction. Google's video gen strategy: accessible pricing to capture market share after OpenAI's Sora shutdown.
[Crescendo: AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**US Chip Export Controls: Sweeping Global Approval Requirement Drafted**
Draft rules would require US government approval to ship AI chips anywhere outside the US — not just China. This follows the Applied Materials $252M fine for illegal exports, the AI OVERWATCH Act (Congressional veto power over chip export licenses), and a 25% tariff on advanced AI chips. The global AI compute map is being redrawn by policy.
[TechCrunch: Sweeping Export Controls](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/us-reportedly-considering-sweeping-new-chip-export-controls/) | [Atlantic Council: Tariffs and Competitiveness](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/new-atlanticist/why-tariffs-on-ai-hardware-could-undermine-us-competitiveness/)

**OpenAI Safety Fellowship Program Announced**
New program for external researchers, engineers, and practitioners to study safety and alignment of advanced AI systems. Comes as the AI safety debate intensifies alongside accelerating model capabilities.
[Crescendo: AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**NVIDIA National Robotics Week: Physical AI Goes Mainstream**
NVIDIA highlighted breakthroughs in sim-to-real robot transfer, foundation models for manipulation, and accelerated training via digital twins. Robots moving from virtual training to real-world deployment faster than ever. Physical AI joins software AI as a 2026 investment thesis.
[NVIDIA: National Robotics Week](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/national-robotics-week-2026/)

## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"AI Is the Only Thing Holding Up the Economy" — Macroeconomic Analysis**
AI-related investments are set to accelerate sharply in 2026, with the surge in AI-driven private-sector spending significantly cushioning the negative impact of tariff hikes. Global trade growth capped at 1.2% in 2026 due to tariff headwinds, but AI-related goods exports account for roughly a third of overall trade growth. Without AI capex, the tariff shock would be devastating.
[Oxford Economics: Trade Outlook](https://www.oxfordeconomics.com/resource/trade-key-themes-2026-global-trade-wedged-between-ai-and-tariffs/) | [Fitch: AI Offsetting Tariffs](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/ai-investment-surge-to-accelerate-in-2026-offsetting-tariff-impact-on-us-economy-fitch-ratings/amp)

**"$242B to AI in One Quarter — Concentration Risk Is the Real Story" — VC Analysis**
Four companies absorbed 65% of all global venture investment. The rest of the AI ecosystem must compete for the remaining 35% of a record-breaking quarter. Analysts warn this concentration creates systemic fragility — if any frontier lab stumbles, the reverberations hit the entire investment category.
[Crunchbase: Foundational AI Funding](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/)

**"Export Controls Are Reshaping the Global Compute Map" — Policy Analysis**
The combination of 25% tariffs on AI chips, draft rules requiring US approval for all chip exports, and sweeping Congressional oversight via AI OVERWATCH is fragmenting the global AI compute landscape. Winners: domestic US manufacturing, sovereign AI buildouts (Mistral, Huawei). Losers: companies dependent on global chip supply chains.
[CSIS: Tariffs vs AI Buildout](https://www.csis.org/analysis/how-tariffs-could-derail-united-states-3-trillion-ai-buildout) | [ML Strategies: 2026 Policy Outlook](https://www.mlstrategies.com/insights-center/viewpoints/54031/2026-02-04-_026-ai-policy-and-semiconductor-outlook-how-federal)

**"Anthropic's $30B Revenue Is the Validation That $852B OpenAI Needed" — Market Commentary**
Anthropic's 3.3x revenue growth in 16 months validates that enterprise AI demand is real and scaling. If Anthropic hits $30B at a $380B valuation, OpenAI's $852B at $25B ARR still looks stretched — but the growth trajectory now has a comparable data point.
[Seeking Alpha: Anthropic Revenue](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4572848-anthropic-targets-30b-revenue-signs-tpu-deal-with-google-and-broadcom)

## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**Gemma 4 26B MoE: 85 tok/s in Just 14 GB**
Updated benchmarks confirm Gemma 4's MoE variant delivers GPT-4 level intelligence in ~14-16 GB — leaving 8-10 GB free for context on a 24GB card. At 85 tok/s, it's the best quality-per-VRAM ratio of any open model. Apache 2.0 license. Native function calling for local agent workflows.
[ToolHalla: Best LLMs for 24GB](https://toolhalla.ai/blog/best-local-llms-24gb-gpu-2026) | [Till Freitag: Open LLM Comparison](https://till-freitag.com/en/blog/open-source-llm-comparison)

**DeepSeek V4: Trillion-Param MoE Tested on OpenRouter Since March**
The largest freely available AI model (1T params, ~37B active) has been running as a stealth test on OpenRouter since March 11. Apache 2.0 weights expected. At 37B active params, GGUF quantizations should fit on 24GB — similar to how Llama 4 Scout's 17B active runs on a 4090.
[Techsy: Best Open-Source LLMs 2026](https://techsy.io/blog/best-open-source-llms-2026)

**Qwen3-30B-A3B Still Holds the Speed Crown: 196 tok/s**
Despite Gemma 4 and DeepSeek V4 joining the roster, the Qwen 3 30B MoE (~3B active) remains the fastest model on RTX 4090 at 196 tok/s. For latency-sensitive agentic loops where speed trumps raw reasoning quality, it's still unbeatable.
[Awesome Agents: GPU Leaderboard](https://awesomeagents.ai/leaderboards/home-gpu-llm-leaderboard/)

**Gemma 4 AWQ INT4: ~15 GB VRAM on RTX 4090**
The AWQ INT4 MoE variant needs ~15GB for model weights, leaving headroom for KV cache. Q4_K_M uses ~16GB. Both fit an RTX 4090 for comfortable development use with room for 32K+ context windows.
[Spheron: Deploy Gemma 4](https://www.spheron.network/blog/deploy-gemma-4-gpu-cloud/)
