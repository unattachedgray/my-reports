## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"AI Is Literally Propping Up the Global Economy" — Trade Economists**
McKinsey Global Institute confirms: despite US tariff rates at their highest since WWII, global trade grew faster than the world economy — because AI-related trade skyrocketed. The tariff war didn't kill trade; AI-driven demand compensated. But this creates a dangerous single point of failure: if AI capex slows, trade has no backstop.
[McKinsey: Geopolitics and Trade](https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/geopolitics-and-the-geometry-of-global-trade-2026-update) | [Euronews: Tariff Winners/Losers](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/03/26/the-biggest-winners-and-losers-of-the-tariff-war-as-ai-related-trade-skyrockets)

**"AI Job Losses Are of a Much Bigger Magnitude Than 2008" — Harvard Economists**
Economists at a Harvard forum warned that threats to the labor market from AI are "of a much bigger magnitude" than the 2008 jobless recovery. The risk: if a recession occurs alongside AI automation, very large job losses could happen simultaneously rather than gradually. This is the economic thesis behind the "Ghost GDP" doom loop argument.
[Harvard Gazette: Economists Weigh AI](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2026/03/economists-weigh-consequences-of-war-tariffs-ai/)

**"Tariffs + AI Spending = Systemic Risk" — Market Analysts**
Analysts note three simultaneous pressures: tariffs squeezing margins, AI spending at potentially unsustainable levels ($650B infrastructure), and consumers facing inflation. All happening at once creates genuine systemic risk. Wall Street year-end targets being downgraded across the board.
[Nasdaq: Market Crash Catalyst](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/forget-president-donald-trumps-tariffs-and-talk-ai-bubble-theres-far-more-sinister)

**"NVIDIA's Hardware Monopoly Is Fracturing" — Industry Analysis**
Meta (MTIA), Huawei (950PR), Google (TPUs for Anthropic), and Amazon (Trainium) are all deploying custom silicon. The trend is unmistakable: every entity spending $10B+ on AI infrastructure is building alternatives to NVIDIA. The question is whether this diversification makes the ecosystem more resilient or fragments optimization.
[Crescendo: AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Self-Hosted AI Agent: VRAM Budget for Agentic Workloads**

Drop to 8-16K context for tool-heavy agents that make many short calls. Gemma 4 26B MoE at 14-16GB leaves the most headroom for agent context.
