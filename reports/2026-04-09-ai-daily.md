# AI Daily Briefing — 2026-04-09

## PART 1 — AI News of the Day

**Claude Mythos: Too Dangerous to Release — Project Glasswing Goes Live**
Anthropic's unreleased frontier model found thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities in every major OS and browser, including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug ($50 in compute) and a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE (CVE-2026-4747). Scored 97.6% on USAMO 2026 math (up from 42.3%). Only 12 partners have access (AWS, Apple, Microsoft, Google, NVIDIA, CrowdStrike, etc.) via Project Glasswing for defensive cybersecurity only. The model will NOT be publicly released.
[TechCrunch: Anthropic Mythos](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/07/anthropic-mythos-ai-model-preview-security/) | [Hacker News: Zero-Days Found](https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/anthropics-claude-mythos-finds.html) | [Anthropic: Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/glasswing)

**April 9 Tariff Chaos: "Reciprocal" Tariffs Hit Then Paused — Except China at 145%**
Sweeping reciprocal tariffs took effect April 9, but most were suspended within hours for 90 days (except a 10% baseline). China excluded from pause — tariffs escalated to 125%, then 145%. Chips and semiconductors exempted from the April 9 wave but separate chip tariffs reportedly in development. Consumer electronics face 25-34% rates on China/Taiwan/Vietnam goods.
[Tax Foundation: Tariff Tracker](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/) | [Tom's Hardware: Chip Exemption](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/trump-administration-exempts-computer-chips-and-copper-from-sweeping-tariffs-but-only-for-now-report-says-chip-tariffs-coming-later)

**Anthropic Revenue Hits $30B Run Rate — 1,000+ Million-Dollar Customers**
Revenue tripled from $9B to $30B run rate in 16 months. Over 1,000 business customers spending $1M+ annually. Combined with the 3.5 GW TPU deal with Google/Broadcom, Anthropic is the fastest-growing enterprise AI company.
[Crescendo: AI News](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Robotics Funding Surge: D-Robotics $150M, Spirit AI $145M**
D-Robotics raised $150M Series B2 (total B round: $270M) for embodied AI. Spirit AI raised $145M for humanoid robot intelligence. Physical AI investment continues accelerating — the embodied intelligence thesis is attracting serious capital.
[AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-startup-funding-news-today/)

**Unsloth Studio: Local Fine-Tuning GUI + Chat Mode**
Unsloth released Studio — a browser-based GUI for local fine-tuning without code. Includes chat mode with side-by-side model comparison, multimodal input (PDFs, images, audio), sandboxed tool calling, and GGUF export. Runs 100% offline on consumer GPUs.
[Unsloth: Studio Intro](https://unsloth.ai/docs/new/studio) | [GitHub: Unsloth](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth)

**AI-Related Trade: Only Growth Engine Standing**
AI-related trade grew ~40% in 2025 vs 6.5% global average. Global trade growth capped at 1.2% in 2026 as tariffs and AI pull in opposite directions. Without AI capex, the world economy would be contracting.
[Oxford Economics: Trade Outlook](https://www.oxfordeconomics.com/resource/trade-key-themes-2026-global-trade-wedged-between-ai-and-tariffs/)

## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"AI Models That Find Zero-Days Faster Than Humans Is the Inflection We Feared" — Security Community**
The security community is split. Some call Glasswing the most important defensive tool since fuzz testing. Others warn that if Mythos-level capability exists at Anthropic, it likely exists (or will soon) at other labs with less caution. The responsible disclosure approach is praised, but the capability genie is out of the bottle — restricting one model doesn't restrict the capability class.
[Simon Willison: Glasswing Commentary](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/7/project-glasswing/) | [The AI Corner: Mythos Analysis](https://www.the-ai-corner.com/p/claude-mythos-preview-project-glasswing-2026)

**"Tariff Pause Changes Nothing — 145% on China Is the Real Story" — Trade Analysts**
The 90-day pause on reciprocal tariffs generated relief rallies, but analysts note the core problem remains: China at 145% disrupts the entire AI supply chain. TSMC, the world's largest chipmaker, manufactures in Taiwan (tariff-affected) and Arizona (tariff-exempt). The arbitrage between these rates will reshape global semiconductor logistics.
[McKinsey: Trade Geometry](https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/geopolitics-and-the-geometry-of-global-trade-2026-update) | [Stanford: Trade War Tracker](https://siepr.stanford.edu/news/scoping-trade-war-new-tool-tracks-company-responses-tariffs-economic-threats)

**"Job Losses of a Much Bigger Magnitude Than 2008" — Harvard Economists**
Harvard economists warn AI automation threats are categorically different from past cycles. If recession coincides with AI-driven workforce restructuring, job losses could be sudden rather than gradual. The Q1 2026 data supports this: 45,000+ tech jobs cut with AI explicitly cited in 20%+.
[Harvard Gazette: AI + Jobs](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2026/03/economists-weigh-consequences-of-war-tariffs-ai/)

**"Trump's Trade War Puts America's AI Ambitions at Risk" — Academic Analysis**
Tariffs at 145% on Chinese goods increase AI development costs and disrupt supply chains for AI hardware. The irony: policies meant to boost US tech leadership may hinder the pace of AI innovation and adoption in the US itself, while accelerating China's push for compute independence (Huawei 950PR).
[The Conversation: Trade War + AI](https://theconversation.com/trumps-trade-war-puts-americas-ai-ambitions-at-risk-254462)

## PART 3 — Home Lab & Hobbyist Corner (Single RTX 4090)

**Unsloth Studio: Fine-Tune Without Code, Chat Locally**
The biggest home lab tool launch this week. Browser-based GUI for local fine-tuning on consumer GPUs. Side-by-side model comparison, multimodal inputs, sandboxed tool calling (web search + code execution), GGUF export to llama.cpp. 100% offline. Supports Gemma 4, Qwen 3.5, DeepSeek, gpt-oss. If you've been intimidated by fine-tuning CLI tools, this is your on-ramp.
[Unsloth Studio](https://unsloth.ai/docs/new/studio) | [HN Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47414372)

**Tariff Impact: GPU Prices Likely Rising — Buy Used Now**
Chips exempted from April 9 tariffs, but separate semiconductor tariffs reportedly coming. Consumer electronics (monitors, peripherals) already hit with 25-34% on China/Taiwan/Vietnam. The used RTX 4090 market is the best value play before prices adjust further. New GPU pricing uncertainty makes used hardware the safer bet.
[Tom's Hardware: Chip Tariffs](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/trump-administration-exempts-computer-chips-and-copper-from-sweeping-tariffs-but-only-for-now-report-says-chip-tariffs-coming-later)

**Gemma 4 26B MoE: Best Quality-Per-VRAM on 4090**
Apache 2.0, 85 tok/s, ~14-16 GB at Q4. Native function calling for local agents. NVIDIA RTX-optimized. The 31B Dense fits at ~20GB for quality-critical work. Both available via Ollama and LM Studio.
[ToolHalla: RTX 4090 Models](https://toolhalla.ai/blog/best-local-llms-rtx-4090-2026)

**LM Studio vs Unsloth Studio: Different Tools, Complementary**
LM Studio remains the default for inference and chat (polished UI, model discovery, API server). Unsloth Studio adds fine-tuning, training monitoring, and GGUF export. Use LM Studio for running models; Unsloth Studio for training and exporting them. Both run locally on 4090.
[CraftRigs: LM Studio vs Unsloth](https://craftrigs.com/comparisons/unsloth-studio-vs-lm-studio-gui-comparison/)
