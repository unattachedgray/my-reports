Announced on World Quantum Day (April 14), Ising is the first family of open-source AI models for quantum computing. Available on GitHub, HuggingFace, and build.nvidia.com.

## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"AI Agents Perform Half as Well as PhD Experts" — Stanford AI Index**
The Nature article and Stanford AI Index provide the most rigorous assessment yet of agentic AI in science. While researchers have embraced AI tools (6-9% of papers reference AI), agents attempting complex multi-step scientific workflows achieve roughly 50% of expert performance. This challenges the "AI replaces scientists" narrative but validates "AI augments scientists" — the actual value proposition.
[Nature: AI vs Scientists](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01199-z)

**"GPT-6 Flat Pricing Was a Preview — Expect the Same for GPT-6" — Industry Analysts**
With open-source models (GLM-5.1, MiniMax M2.7, Qwen Coder 32B) matching GPT-5.4 on coding at $0, analysts predict GPT-6 will also launch at aggressive pricing. The model capabilities race is becoming a pricing race. Whoever can deliver frontier quality at the lowest per-token cost wins — and open-source has already set the floor at zero.
[FindSkill: GPT-6](https://findskill.ai/blog/gpt-6-release-date/)

**"Capital Flowing to Instrumentation, Not Models" — VC Commentary**
April 14 funding patterns confirm the trend: startups that instrument AI systems (observability, orchestration, security) are attracting growth-stage capital faster than model builders. The insight: the model layer is commoditizing; value is migrating to the infrastructure that manages, secures, and governs models in production.
[AlleyWatch: Funding Trends](https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/04/the-alleywatch-startup-daily-funding-report-4-14-2026/)

**"Claude Cowork Moving Stock Prices Is a Milestone" — Market Observers**
The legal tech stock sell-off following Claude Cowork's launch is the first time a model feature announcement has directly moved sector equities. This signals that AI product launches are now material market events — not just tech news. Expect future model releases to require investor relations coordination, similar to drug approvals moving pharma stocks.

While primarily for quantum researchers, the Ising Calibration model (VLM-based) runs on standard GPU infrastructure. Not a daily-use tool for most home labs, but a significant open-source addition.
