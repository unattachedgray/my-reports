China remains the world's largest producer of the critical electrical components, creating a tariff-supply chain paradox.

## PART 2 — Expert Analysis & Commentary

**"The Trust Gap Is Now Critical" — Stanford AI Index Analysis**
73% of AI experts think AI will positively impact jobs. Only 23% of the public agrees. This 50-point gap is the widest in the report's history and represents a fundamental governance challenge: policies designed by optimistic experts may not address the fears of the people affected. Transparency is making it worse — major labs are disclosing less, not more.
[eWeek: Trust Gap](https://www.eweek.com/news/stanford-ai-index-2026-trust-gap-neuron/) | [Stanford HAI: Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report)

**"Data Center Delays Are the Real AI Bottleneck — Not Models" — Infrastructure Analysts**
With half of planned capacity delayed and transformer lead times at 5 years, the $650B AI infrastructure buildout faces a physical constraint that no amount of funding can immediately solve. The irony: tariffs on Chinese electrical equipment make the shortage worse, while the AI buildout that tariffs are supposed to support requires exactly those components.
[TechRadar: Supply Chain Crisis](https://www.techradar.com/pro/if-one-piece-of-your-supply-chain-is-delayed-then-your-whole-project-cant-deliver-nearly-half-of-us-data-centers-planned-for-2026-canceled-or-delayed-and-things-could-soon-get-much-worse)

**"China Closed the Gap Without Matching the Spend" — Geopolitical Analysis**
Stanford's finding that Chinese models trail Anthropic by just 2.7% despite far lower private investment challenges the assumption that AI leadership correlates with capital deployment. Chinese efficiency (DeepSeek V4 at $5.2M training cost, Huawei's non-NVIDIA compute) suggests the US "outspend to win" strategy may not hold.
[SiliconANGLE: China Neck-and-Neck](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/13/stanford-hais-2026-ai-index-reveals-china-u-s-now-neck-neck-race-global-dominance/) | [MLQ: China Eliminates Lead](https://mlq.ai/news/stanfords-2026-ai-index-reveals-china-nearly-eliminates-us-performance-lead/)

**"GenAI Value Per User Tripled — But Only 20% of Companies Capture 75%" — Economic Analysis**
The median value of generative AI per user tripled between 2025 and 2026, reaching $172B annually for US consumers alone. But PwC's finding that 20% of companies capture 75% of gains means this value is highly concentrated. The gap between AI-savvy organizations and laggards is becoming the defining economic divide of 2026.
[PwC: AI Performance Study](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-performance-study.html) | [Stanford: AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report)
