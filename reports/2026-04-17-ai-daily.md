# AI Daily Briefing — 2026-04-17 (Friday)

## PART 1 — AI News of the Day

**Claude Opus 4.7 Released Yesterday — 87.6% SWE-bench, 3x Vision, Same Price**
Anthropic shipped Opus 4.7 on April 16 with a massive 7-point SWE-bench jump (80.8% → 87.6%), leapfrogging both GPT-5.4 (57.7%) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (80.6%) on the harder SWE-bench Pro variant (64.3%). Vision resolution tripled to 2,576px (3.75 megapixels). New "xhigh" effort level for finer reasoning-vs-latency control. 1M token context. Pricing unchanged: $5/$25 per million tokens. Available on Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry.
[Anthropic: Introducing Opus 4.7](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7) | [The Next Web: Opus 4.7 Benchmarks](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-claude-opus-4-7-coding-agentic-benchmarks-release) | [FindSkill: Opus 4.7 Review](https://findskill.ai/blog/claude-opus-4-7-review/)

**Mozilla Launches Thunderbolt — Open-Source, Self-Hosted Enterprise AI Client**
Mozilla's MZLA Technologies released Thunderbolt on April 16, an open-source (MPL 2.0) AI client for enterprises wanting to run self-hosted AI. Supports any model (commercial, open-source, local), connects via Haystack framework, MCP servers, and Agent Client Protocol. Native apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android. Positioned as the "sovereign" alternative to proprietary AI stacks.
[Phoronix: Mozilla Thunderbolt](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Mozilla-Thunderbolt) | [The Register: Mozilla Enterprise AI](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/16/mozilla_thunderbolt_enterprise_ai_client)

**Fortune: "Most of You Are Rejecting AI — You're Running Out of Time"**
A Fortune analysis finds widespread AI resistance in the workplace despite executive mandates. Stanford AI Index confirms the gap: 73% of experts positive on AI's job impact vs 23% of public. Among Gen Z, excitement dropped from 36% to 22% year-over-year, while anger rose from 22% to 31%. The adoption pressure is intensifying even as trust erodes.
[Fortune: AI Resistance](https://fortune.com/2026/04/16/ai-resistance-running-out-of-time-rebellion-quiet-quitting-trust/)

**Stanford AI Index: AI Data Centers Now Consume Switzerland-Level Power**
AI data center capacity hit 29.6 GW in 2026, comparable to Switzerland's national electricity consumption. Yet half of planned US capacity is delayed or canceled due to transformer shortages. The energy footprint of AI is becoming a first-order policy concern, with Sanders/AOC proposing a federal moratorium on new data centers.
[Stanford HAI: Full Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report) | [EdTech Innovation Hub: Stanford Findings](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/ai-adoption-accelerates-as-stanford-report-highlights-pressure-on-education-jobs-and-infrastructure)

**Manycore Tech IPO: +187% on Hong Kong Debut**
AI training data company pivoting to supply robot makers surged 187% on its Hong Kong debut after raising $156M. The AI data supply chain is becoming its own investment category as demand for high-quality training data intensifies.

**27 States Advancing Data Center Legislation**
State laws requiring developers to cover energy costs and report usage are spreading fast. California, Ohio, Utah already enacted. Maine poised for first construction moratorium (until Nov 2027). Federal vs. state tension escalating as the White House pushes infrastructure expansion while states push back.
